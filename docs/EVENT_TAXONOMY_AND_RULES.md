## 2. Event Types & Rule Specifications

### 2.1 `POSSIBLE_FALL` (Tier 3 — Urgent Review)
- **Problem Statement**: Detect when an individual experiences an abrupt collapse or slip, remaining prone on the floor requiring medical or security assistance.
- **Observable Cues Evaluated**:
  1. **Vertical Centroid Velocity**: Centroid $Y$ increases rapidly downward ($\Delta Y / \Delta t > 350 \text{ px/s}$).
  2. **Bounding Box Aspect Ratio Inversion**: Ratio $\text{Height} / \text{Width}$ transitions from $> 1.8$ (upright posture) to $< 0.9$ (ground-level horizontal posture).
  3. **Ground Stillness Period**: Post-drop velocity drops below $30 \text{ px/s}$ and sustains for $\ge 3.0 \text{ seconds}$.
- **Evidence Formulation**:
  > *"Possible fall observed. Subject #12 exhibited rapid vertical level drop followed by sustained horizontal ground posture (3.8s). Immediate assistance check recommended."*

---

### 2.2 `POSSIBLE_ALTERCATION` (Tier 2/3 — Review)
- **Problem Statement**: Flag anomalous multi-person dynamic confrontations without making assumptions about intent or weapon presence.
- **Observable Cues Evaluated**:
  1. **Rapid Convergence**: Two or more person tracks approach each other with closing speed $> 400 \text{ px/s}$.
  2. **Bounding Box Proximity & Overlap**: Distance between centroids $< 0.8 \times (\text{Width}_A + \text{Width}_B)$, or IoU intersection $> 0.20$.
  3. **Erratic Velocity & Directional Variance**: High angular acceleration in bounding box trajectories over consecutive frames ($\ge 6 \text{ direction reversals in 1.5s}$).
  4. **Multi-Track Dwell**: The cluster remains tightly locked for $> 1.5 \text{ seconds}$.
- **Evidence Formulation**:
  > *"Possible physical altercation observed. Tracks #04 and #09 converged at high velocity with sustained bounding box overlap and erratic trajectory shifts. Please review camera."*

---

### 2.3 `UNATTENDED_ITEM` (Tier 1/2 — Observation / Review)
- **Problem Statement**: Detect when a carried item (backpack, briefcase, box) is detached and left behind while people depart.
- **Observable Cues Evaluated**:
  1. **Stationary Dwell**: Prop bounding box centroid movement $< 8 \text{ px}$ over $\ge 30 \text{ seconds}$.
  2. **Carrier Separation**: Nearest detected person track distance increases beyond $250 \text{ px}$.
  3. **No Co-located Owner**: No person is detected within the interaction radius for $> 45 \text{ seconds}$.
- **Evidence Formulation**:
  > *"Unattended item observed. Object #08 (backpack) has remained stationary for 45s with no person within 250px radius."*

---

### 2.4 `ZONE_INTRUSION` (Tier 2 — Review)
- **Problem Statement**: Detect unauthorized entry into designated sensitive areas (server rack, loading dock, restricted threshold).
- **Observable Cues Evaluated**:
  1. **Ground Anchor Test**: The person's bottom-center coordinate $(X_{\text{center}}, Y_{\text{bottom}})$ falls inside the user-defined polygon vertices.
  2. **Schedule Filter**: Zone is flagged as active for the current time of day.
- **Evidence Formulation**:
  > *"Restricted zone entry. Track #03 entered polygon 'Server Enclosure A' at 11:42:10Z."*

---

### 2.5 `CROWDING_THRESHOLD` (Tier 1/2 — Observation / Review)
- **Problem Statement**: Monitor area capacity limits for safety and bottleneck prevention.
- **Observable Cues Evaluated**:
  1. **Occupancy Count**: Simultaneous active person tracks within zone boundaries $\ge \text{CapacityThreshold}$.
  2. **Sustain Window**: Threshold exceeded continuously for $\ge 5.0 \text{ seconds}$.
- **Evidence Formulation**:
  > *"Crowding alert. Zone 'East Corridor' occupancy reached 9 persons (threshold: 6)."*

## 3. Cooldown & Deduplication Matrix

To prevent alert fatigue and redundant notifications:
- **Event Debounce Window**: If an active event of the same type is occurring for the same track ID, subsequent frames append evidence to the existing event record rather than generating duplicate events.
- **Cooldown Timers**:
  - `POSSIBLE_FALL`: 60 seconds per track.
  - `POSSIBLE_ALTERCATION`: 30 seconds per cluster.
  - `UNATTENDED_ITEM`: 120 seconds per item.
  - `ZONE_INTRUSION`: 15 seconds per track.
